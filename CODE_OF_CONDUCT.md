# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or publishing other people's private information.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or open a private security advisory addressed to the maintainers.
